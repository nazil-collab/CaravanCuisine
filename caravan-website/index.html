About Caravan

Arabic cuisine, the Caravan way

For eight years, Caravan has brought the slow-grilled, generously spiced food of the Arabian kitchen to Thalassery. Whole chickens marinated and kissed by charcoal. Mandi rice slow-cooked in aromatic broth. Shawarmas, wraps and loaded fries, with fresh juices, mojitos and creamy Arabian shakes to cool it all down. Made the same way, every single time.
